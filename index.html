Meet Our Talent

The Faces Behind the Stories

Meet the talent bringing our stories to life.

Milica

ACTRESS · RHYTHMIC GYMNAST · PIANIST · MODEL · EQUESTRIAN SPORTS

Milica is a charismatic and vibrant young actress whose positive energy brings warmth to every set. Known for her quick wit and playful sense of humour, she loves making the cast and crew laugh. Yet when the cameras start rolling, she knows how to focus and deliver emotionally compelling performances.

Having been involved in acting from a very young age, Milica has developed a natural understanding of the craft — something that shines through in her work.

“I absolutely love the filmmaking process. I find it incredibly fascinating to watch everything gradually come together and turn into a truly amazing story — especially knowing that you're part of creating it!” — MILICA

Alice

ASPIRING ACTRESS · RHYTHMIC GYMNAST · TENNIS PLAYER · ASPIRING MODEL

Alice is a kind, friendly, and thoughtful young actress who brings a calm and welcoming presence to the set. Her natural connection with her fellow cast members, including Milica, creates an atmosphere where creativity and collaboration can thrive.

Although Alice is at the beginning of her acting journey, she already shows a wonderful ability to immerse herself in her roles. We are excited to see her grow, explore new challenges, and continue developing her craft.

“I really enjoy being part of this project and working with such a friendly team. This is my first acting project, and I'm so happy that this is the one I get to begin my journey with!” — ALICE
